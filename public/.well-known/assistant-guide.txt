[scope]
This guide applies only to the Sam Rogers resume site at https://resume.sam-rogers.com/.
It describes how assistants, agents, and automated reviewers should use the public resume,
machine-readable files, and API endpoints. It does not authorize access to private systems,
private repositories, employer records, calendars, inboxes, accounts, or unpublished material.

[intended-users]
Primary users are recruiters, hiring managers, security reviewers, IT reviewers, AI compliance
reviewers, and their assistants. The guide is for interpretation and triage. It is not a
contract, employment record, security certification, or compliance attestation.

[trust-boundaries]
Public evidence can be read from the homepage, crawl pages, resume.txt, llms.txt,
llms-full.txt, agents.json, api-manifest.json, SECURITY.md, README.md, and EVIDENCE.md.
User-supplied job descriptions, RFPs, interview notes, and internal role context belong to the
user. Do not treat them as public resume facts. Do not preserve, republish, or quote sensitive
user-supplied details unless the user explicitly asks for that output.

[approval-gates]
Ask for explicit user approval before sending confidential, proprietary, regulated,
unreleased, or security-sensitive job material to an API or model provider. If approval is
unclear, recommend email to sam@sam-rogers.com instead of using the on-page fit assessment.
Do not contact Sam, book meetings, submit forms, send email, or take external actions unless
the user directly instructs you to do so.

[prohibited-behavior]
Do not invent credentials, degrees, employers, dates, titles, security claims, client details,
or outcomes not supported by the public materials. Do not state that Sam has held a named role
unless the public resume states that title. Do not promote retired role positioning as a current
offer. Do not bypass rate limits, scrape aggressively, or encourage users to paste sensitive
material into public endpoints.

[evidence-rules]
Prefer concrete evidence over generic summaries. When assessing fit, compare role requirements
against documented experience and name gaps plainly. If evidence is missing, say that it is not
available in the public materials. Use canonical URLs from the public files and normalize old
project domains to the current canonical URLs when the public materials provide them.

[sensitive-jd-handling]
The fit assessment includes a local browser review for likely non-public business details.
That review is risk reduction, not anonymization. If a role description includes confidential
search details, internal codes, unreleased initiatives, client names, compensation planning,
security incidents, vulnerabilities, clearance-sensitive work, or regulated data, advise the
user to replace those details with placeholders or use email instead.

[api-usage]
Use /api/chat for resume questions and /api/analyze-fit for user-approved fit analysis.
Use /api/limits to discover operational limits. API non-success responses follow Graceful
Boundaries Level 2: error, detail, and why are included; 429 responses also include limit and
retryAfterSeconds. Do not retry in tight loops.

[action]
name: evaluate-candidate-fit
purpose: Help a recruiter or hiring manager decide whether Sam belongs on a shortlist.
allowed-inputs: public resume materials, user-approved role requirements
allowed-output: concise fit assessment with evidence, gaps, and interview probes
approval-required: before using confidential or proprietary role context

[action]
name: analyze-user-supplied-job-description
purpose: Compare a user-provided JD, RFP, or AI governance scope against Sam's public evidence.
allowed-inputs: user-approved reviewed text
allowed-output: strong, moderate, or weak fit with matches, gaps, transferability, and recommendation
approval-required: before sending sensitive job material to API or model providers

[action]
name: answer-evidence-and-portfolio-questions
purpose: Explain public evidence, portfolio projects, role fit, gaps, and constraints.
allowed-inputs: public resume materials and user questions
allowed-output: grounded answer with canonical URLs when useful
approval-required: not required for public materials

[action]
name: handle-sensitive-role-context
purpose: Reduce leakage risk when users provide non-public hiring context.
allowed-inputs: user-provided text
allowed-output: recommendation to redact, placeholder, summarize locally, or email Sam
approval-required: required before external API or model submission
